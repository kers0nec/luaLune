LuaLune Protection Engine

Protect your Luau without the ugly stuff.

Paste or drop your source, pick an engine, and get a fresh per-build loader. The LuaLune Obfuscator encrypts your payload with a new key every time; the Flow engine flattens control flow and rewrites identifiers, strings and numbers.

01

LuaLune Obfuscator

Randomized payload encryption, shuffled chunk delivery, an integrity check and a runtime decoder that rebuilds your chunk in memory.

02

Flow & Vault

Flow flattens control flow and rewrites identifiers, strings and numbers. Vault cascades three transforms with per-segment keys, decoy records and integrity checks.

03

Delivery built in

Stable loader URLs, permanent or timed keys, HWID whitelists, execution logs and workspace sharing.

Never breaks your script

Each pass verifies the build, and anything that cannot be transformed safely is skipped with a warning instead of shipping a broken script.

Gold-standard accounts

Email or username sign-in, sessions, a human check on signup and per-plan usage limits.

Honest promises

Obfuscation raises the cost of reading your source. It is not encryption of secrets — nothing that must stay confidential belongs in a script.
